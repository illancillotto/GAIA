## Behavioral invariants and approved design

Compute positive liters once, positive means once instead of three calls,
and pure target once per vehicle. Typed immutable computational result carries
consumption, coefficient and judgement only; no generic context or SQL.
Reuse existing judgement logic for vehicle/operator classification, counting
its relocation as neutral rather than claiming it as improvement.

Preserve raw positive liters / distance * 100 arithmetic order, consumption
rounding to 2 decimals, classify raw coefficient before rounding to 3,
price/cost/liter mean precisions 4/2/2, existing nullable conventions and
unrounded distance. Preserve net-liters ranking, input tie order, all query
predicates/ordering, stations and related output, and canonical identities.
Absent distance is distinct from a present zero distance in response output.
Missing cost is excluded from positive-cost mean but its liters remain in
price-per-liter denominator. Storno/zero fuel affect net totals, not positive
means/consumption. The vehicle-type classifier is pure; evaluating it once
rather than repeatedly/conditionally has no observable side effect.

Nonfinite contracts: NaN does not satisfy `> 0`; infinity arithmetic remains
unchanged. Coefficient NaN fails both `<=` checks and keeps existing
`Molto alto` judgement. Target values continue to come from the unchanged
classifier, including its current `autocarro`/`auto` substring precedence.

## Characterization tests

R2 79 cases remain unchanged; 10 new SQL-backed finite response cases cover
raw-versus-rounded judgement boundaries, sub-rounding positive values,
mixed storno/zero/missing costs, absent/zero/negative odometer-derived distance,
and net ranking with ties. Characterization precedes runtime mutation.
NaN/infinity cannot be faithfully persisted through SQLite Numeric fixtures;
direct pure-computation tests will compare the existing arithmetic contract
without fabricated ORM/query results.

factory converts cost using Decimal(str(cost)) and rejects None before route
execution. Corrected fixture creates a valid log then assigns nullable
`total_cost=None` before flush. Runtime remains unchanged during correction.
No production behavior was fixed, and the failed run is not a PASS claim.
