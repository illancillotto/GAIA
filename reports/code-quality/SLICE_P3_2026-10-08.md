## Behavioral invariants

- Date bounds are inclusive; fuel sessions exclude open status.
- Route distance wins over odometer distance; negative odometer distance is clamped.
- All logs contribute to total liters/cost and counts, including storni and zero liters.
- Positive-only logs determine refuel means; null cost does not enter cost means.
- Existing substring precedence is retained: `autocarro` currently matches `auto`
  before the truck rule and has a 7.5 target. This is characterized, not corrected.
- Coefficient judgement is based on its unrounded value; response coefficient is rounded.
- Canonical WC-to-GAIA linkage takes precedence over legacy session name variants.
- Operator totals consolidate known WC display-name variants, while vehicle-related
  operator keys remain unconsolidated as in current behavior.
- Empty/malformed dates use fallbacks; unavailable identity remains unknown.
- Missing or negative session durations do not become duration extrema.
